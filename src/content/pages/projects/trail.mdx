Trail is an Obsidian plugin for managing named relations between notes and traversing them through a dedicated pane. I developed it to support complex ontologies inside Obsidian vaults. It supports complex inference rules, implied relations, etc. It is meant to be an improvement over plugins like "Breadcrumbs". 

Currently, the development is paused.
