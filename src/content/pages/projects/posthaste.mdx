*Your email, delivered at Posthaste.*

Posthaste is an OSS modular email workstation. As a regular client, it is very fast, friendly and powerful, but it can also be deployed in various configurations, subscribed to as a daemon over documented API, and integrated with agents via an MCP. 

Posthaste is easy to use, easy to install, and easy to make yours. You can check out Posthaste at [posthaste.theor.net](posthaste.theor.net).
