## 1. Who Is?

I aspire to build & research to empower good agents of all kinds! I believe in a friendly, free, abundant world and want to help accelerate its arrival through thought and praxis: 

- I have been a founding member of the technical team at [Palisade](https://palisaderesearch.org/) 
- built [Posthaste](/projects/posthaste), [Trail](/projects/trail), Cue, [Blank](/projects/blank), this website,
- practiced theatre and dance professionaly,
- created a large TTRPG community in Tbilisi and helped organise a thematic festival, 
- got accepted into [Surplus](https://surplus.dev/) solo, pre-idea,  
- have been a JC at [ESPR](https://espr.camp/) by [FABRIC](https://www.fabric.camp/),
- published on NeurIPS with ["Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01784), 

and did other exciting things during this pursuit!
