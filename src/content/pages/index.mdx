## 1. Who Is?

I aspire to build & research to empower good agents of all kinds! I believe in a friendly, free, abundant world and want to help accelerate its arrival through thought and praxis: 

- I joined [Palisade](https://palisaderesearch.org/) as a founding member of the technical team & left it two years later
- built (WIP) [Posthaste](https://posthaste.theor.net/), Trail, Cue, Blank, 
- in school, did theatre and dance semi-professionaly,
- created a large TTRPG community in Tbilisi and organised a fest, 
- got accepted into [Surplus](https://surplus.dev/) solo, 
- became a JC at [ESPR](https://espr.camp/) by [FABRIC](https://www.fabric.camp/),
- published on NeurIPS with ["Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01784), 
- played a lot of computer games, read less books and watched less movies (unfortunately)

and did other exciting things during this pursuit.
